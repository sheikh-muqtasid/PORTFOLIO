### Overview

Dental Suite is a modern clinic management platform designed specifically for dental professionals. It centralizes patient data, scheduling, and treatment history into a secure, user-friendly interface that streamlines administrative workflows and improves patient care.

**Technologies**: React.js, MERN Stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js)  
**Project Type**: Healthcare Workflow Management

### Key Features

- **Patient Record Management**: Structured storage and management for treatment history and medical records.
- **Appointment Scheduling**: Real-time scheduling with clear daily and weekly views.
- **Secure Data Handling**: Robust security features for managing sensitive patient information.
- **Intuitive UI**: Simple, effective interface built with doctors and clinic staff in mind.
- **Fast Retrieval**: Optimized search and retrieval for efficient access to patient histories.

### Role & Contributions

- **Full-Stack Development**: Engineered both the React frontend and Node.js/Express backend.
- **Data Architecture**: Designed the MongoDB schema for complex patient and appointment relationships.
- **UI Design**: Crafted a clear, professional interface focused on healthcare productivity.
- **Security Implementation**: Developed secure authentication and data protection layers.

### Impact

- **Operational Efficiency**: Reduced administrative burden for dental clinics.
- **Enhanced Patient Care**: Improved clinical decision-making with easy access to full patient history.
- **Digital Transformation**: Successfully modernized traditional clinic record-keeping.
